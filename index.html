PROJECT OVERVIEW
Turning mechanical movement into measurable electrical energy

VibeVolt explores how movement and vibration from a model train can stress piezoelectric elements and produce electrical charge. An appropriate conditioning circuit can collect and manage this output, while an ESP32 can eventually transmit measured data to the web dashboard.

⇢

01 · Mechanical system

A movable pressure plate supported by four springs responds to the model train's movement. Travel stops help protect the piezo discs from excessive force.

ϟ

02 · Energy conversion

Piezoelectric discs generate electrical charge when stressed. A separate DC generator may provide another source through a suitable mechanical linkage.

⌘

03 · Smart monitoring

An ESP32 can collect conditioned sensor measurements and send them through the internet to display real experimental data in this app.
